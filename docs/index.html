What the wheel does

  • Link or create notes. Search your vault, or create a note with an editable title. Your wording is kept as the link's text.
  • Toggle formatting. Bold, italic, highlight, strikethrough, and your own wrappers such as %%comment%%.
  • Extract a passage. Move a selection into a new note that links back to its source.
  • Define. Wikipedia and Wiktionary look-ups, and one click to turn the summary into a note.
  • Link tools. Over an existing link: preview it, unlink it, point it elsewhere, or open it in a split.
  • After linking. Save your wording as an alias, or link every other mention in the note at once.
